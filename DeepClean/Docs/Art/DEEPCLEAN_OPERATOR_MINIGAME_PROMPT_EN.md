## Copy-ready prompt

Create a clear industrial game-prop concept sheet for a SMALL RAIL-MOUNTED MAINTENANCE CARRIAGE on the referenced boat. This is a functional proposal for DEEPCLEAN, a cozy underwater reclamation game. Preserve the rusty blue boat's material identity and small scale. Do not redesign the vessel. Focus on the mechanism rather than an atmospheric panorama.

Show one dominant close three-quarter view and two small supporting views of the SAME mechanism: partly opened and fully retracted. Use consistent parts, dimensions and orientation across views. Render with believable worn painted metal, clear silhouettes and soft neutral lighting. Minimal background; a small patch of deck and existing service opening establishes context. Do not invent additional ship decks, cabins or hull openings. Placement near an existing service opening is provisional, not a measured layout.

MECHANICAL STRUCTURE
A rectangular maintenance carriage slides sideways on TWO parallel fixed guide rails to uncover an existing service access opening. The carriage contains a compact industrial service housing, not a treasure chest. In the closed state, it physically blocks entry; in the fully retracted state, the opening is unobstructed and the carriage rests in a clear parking space beside it.

A fixed, protected gearbox drives a visible horizontal lead screw parallel to the rails. A captive nut attached to the carriage converts screw rotation into linear carriage motion. The shaft has fixed supports. Show the mechanical relationship clearly: fixed supports, rotating screw, moving nut, moving carriage. The mechanism is self-locking, so an ordinary pull on the carriage does not open it.

The fixed gearbox has ONE accessible recessed keyed service socket. A future tool attachment supplies rotary drive through this socket. The socket is a robust mechanical coupling, not a glowing keyhole, touchscreen or magical lock. No human hands or player weapon design is required.

GAMEPLAY STATES
In the main partly opened view, the carriage has moved enough to expose a previously covered maintenance gap. A separate short bent metal wedge is trapped between a rail stop and the carriage, preventing further forward motion. Make both contact points readable. The wedge must be physically reachable through the exposed gap only after partial opening.

Reversing the carriage a short distance releases pressure on the wedge without completely covering the gap. The player can then remove the loose wedge using an existing grab tool. Forward drive subsequently retracts the carriage completely and opens access. The supporting final view shows the wedge removed and the clear opening.

The wedge is an obstruction, not a collectible key. Keep the construction simple enough that this sequence is understandable visually. Do not add multiple color locks, extra puzzle wheels, cranes, hydraulic circuits or unrelated machines. Do not depict the carriage floating freely away from its rails.

ENVIRONMENTAL DETAILS
Include small removable patches of granular residue near the rail and thin viscous grime on a corner, but keep the screw, socket and obstruction visible for design review. Retain the reference's worn blue paint and rust, with a restrained contrasting service accent. Heavy parts have physical supports. Underwater context must not obscure mechanical readability with fog, bloom or particles.

OUTPUT
One wide concept sheet. Use clean spacing between the views. No decorative typography or long labels. The images must show a consistent functional object, not three unrelated designs. Do not include invented franchise logos, HUD, human figures, keys or energy barriers. Prioritize mechanical continuity over cinematic detail.
