Retain the original hull and cabin. Add only three coherent modular assemblies, scaled for this small working boat:
1. A compact bolted A-frame lifting gantry and winch at an accessible working-deck edge. Show structural feet, a cable drum and a lifting line attached to a plausible load. Keep the deck usable and the cabin recognizable.
2. A short guided core-handling/extraction cradle at an existing deck hatch. It holds a thick cylindrical sampling barrel with a partly exposed mineral core. Show a guide rail, two or three substantial clamp collars, and packed residue around one collar. Orient this assembly so its shape can be read from the selected camera. This equipment is a maintenance and recovery target: an interrupted process awaiting clearing and release, not a drill magically boring through the ship's hull.

THREE-DIMENSIONAL UNDERWATER CLUTTER — ESSENTIAL

Show an irregular loose volume of lightweight objects drifting at several heights: just above the seabed, around player height and near the boat's working-deck edge.
